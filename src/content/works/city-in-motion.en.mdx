## On the edit

Twelve hours of footage from the first train to the last, cut down to four minutes and twelve seconds. The film moves to the rhythm of the metro: doors opening, pulling away, going through tunnels, with every transition landing on a beat.

## Production notes

Colour was graded in DaVinci Resolve, pushing the night scenes toward teal. The slow shots were filmed at 60fps and dropped onto a 24fps timeline, exactly two and a half times slower. Anything static longer than three seconds came out.
