## About the film

A twelve-square-metre room that holds a shooting table, a retouching desk and a woodworking bench at the same time. Shot over three months and cut to twelve minutes and forty-five seconds, the film is built around sound: shutter, sanding, keyboard.

Most of it was filmed on a 50mm prime with window light only, so the ISO sat around 1600 the whole time. A single shotgun mic, hidden at the corner of the desk.

Editing was done in Premiere Pro. There is no score, only room tone.
