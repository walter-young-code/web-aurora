## About the shoot

First trip to the salt flats in early June. At noon there are no shadows, and the ground is white enough to lose all detail. Past the evaporation line the horizon simply disappears; standing in it you have no sense of direction, only your own shadow still pointing west.

The kit was small: a 24mm lens, a polariser to knock the glare off the salt, f/11 at ISO 100, aperture priority. Where the water was thin I let the shutter drop to a quarter of a second, and the crust texture blends into its own reflection until you cannot tell them apart.

## Afterword

Four frames survived when I got home. The rest were too full. Empty space, photographed too much, stops looking empty.
