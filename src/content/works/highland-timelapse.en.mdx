## About the shoot

Three days at a pass around 4,000 metres, waiting on cloud, light and one late snowfall. The interval was set to one frame every four seconds on the camera's built-in timelapse; battery by day, power bank by night.

## The film

Out of more than twenty thousand frames, nine sequences survived. They were graded together in Lightroom and stitched into a two-and-a-half-minute film. LRTimelapse handled the exposure transitions and cleaned up the flicker; the snow sequence keeps a little shake, which reads as wind.
