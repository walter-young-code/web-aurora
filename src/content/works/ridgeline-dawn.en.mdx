## The shoot

Went up in mid-May, leaving the saddle at four in the morning with a headlamp, close to an hour of walking before the ridge. The sky had not lit yet, and the fog in the valley below was thick enough to hide the trail.

## Light and gear

For the first twenty minutes after sunrise the light came in sideways through gaps in the clouds, and the ridge was nothing but an edge line. Camera on the tripod, 35mm lens at f/8, ISO 100, exposure pulled two thirds of a stop down for the highlights. The fog cleared slowly; the three frames are barely ten minutes apart.
