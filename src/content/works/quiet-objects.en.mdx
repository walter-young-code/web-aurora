## About the shoot

A table of everyday objects, arranged by the window and shot over two afternoons. No lamps, only the low side light from the west-facing window, which holds best between four and five.

Once the layout was fixed, I waited for the light to touch the rim of the cup before releasing the shutter. 50mm macro at f/4, ISO 200, handheld with the body resting on a support stick. The light moves faster than you expect; five minutes apart, the same frame throws a completely different shadow.

This set is still being expanded. The frame that best explains the light is on the cover.
